# Market Research

Use this skill when a user asks for market context, competitor notes, or a short research-backed business brief.

Start by clarifying the target market, time horizon, and geography when they are missing. Prefer concise source-backed findings and use generated HTML or CSV artifacts when the user asks for a deliverable.
